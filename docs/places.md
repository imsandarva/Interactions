# Places

Study 03. A list of places, and the card that becomes the page.

The home row reads Places. Inside, six quiet places sit in a single column. Tap a card and that same card grows to fill the screen. The picture, the title, and the line under it travel with it. Drag the page down, tap Close, or use the system back gesture, and it shrinks back into the card it left, in the same spot.

Pulling down only dismisses when the page is already at the top. A scroll that reaches the top keeps scrolling. Scrolling and dismissing stay out of each other's way.

Save this place presses, and that is all. Nothing is kept.

The pictures are painted tones until real photos are added. Set `photo` on a place in `lib/studies/places/copy/places.dart` to an asset path, and declare that asset in `pubspec.yaml`.

Timings and distances live in `lib/studies/places/motion/places_spec.dart`.
