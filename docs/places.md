# Places

Study 03. A list of places, and the card that becomes the page.

The home row reads Places. Inside, six quiet places sit in a single column. Tap a card and that same card grows to fill the screen. The picture, the title, and the line under it travel with it, and the white panel under the picture becomes the page. Drag the page, tap Close, or use the system back gesture, and it shrinks back into the card it left, in the same spot.

Each place has three photographs. Swipe across the picture, or tap either side, to move between them. The card keeps the one you were just looking at.

Pulling down only dismisses when the page is already at the top. Once it is moving, it follows your finger. A scroll that reaches the top keeps scrolling. Scrolling, paging, and dismissing stay out of each other's way.

Save this place turns into Saved. When the card lands, a small mark settles on the photograph. The list remembers the mark, and which photograph you left on, for as long as the app is open.

Each page takes on a faint color from its place. The button uses a deeper version of that same color.

The photographs live in `assets/places/`. Timings and distances live in `lib/studies/places/motion/places_spec.dart`.
