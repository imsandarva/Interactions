# Payment

Study 01. A subscription moment for Plus.

The screen behind the button is Studio: a quiet list of things that stay locked. A dark Pay now pill sits at the bottom.

Tapping the pill turns that same pill into the checkout sheet. It grows to about three quarters of the screen, the color warms to paper while it is still moving, and the offer fades in only once there is room. Closing does the reverse: the offer leaves first, then the sheet shrinks back into the pill.

Inside the sheet, yearly is already chosen. Monthly is the same kind of card, not a lesser one. The highlight slides between them. The button reads Start yearly · $50 or Start monthly · $6. It presses, and that is all for now.

Drag the sheet down, tap the dimmed page, tap Close, or use the system back gesture. All of them return to the pill. A drag can also reverse an opening that is still in motion.

Timings and sizes live in `lib/studies/payment/motion/pay_timing.dart` and `pay_frame.dart`.
