# Feedback

Study 02. How an experience felt, answered with one face.

The home row reads Feedback. Inside, a dark pill reads Give us feedback. Tapping it raises a sheet about half the screen tall.

The sheet asks how the experience was. A face of two eyes and a mouth rests at Fine. Drag the slider and that same face bends from Bad through Fine to Great, and the sheet's color follows: coral, then butter, then sage. Let go and the thumb settles on the nearest stop.

Send stays quiet until the slider is touched. Then it fills in. Tapping it reads Thanks, and the sheet lowers. Nothing is saved. A follow-up question is a later study.

Timings and sizes live in `lib/studies/feedback/motion/feedback_spec.dart`. The three faces live in `lib/studies/feedback/face/face_pose.dart`. The color path lives in `lib/studies/feedback/theme/mood_palette.dart`.
